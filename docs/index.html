Analyze a customer dataset

Upload a CSV file with one row per customer and a column that shows whether the customer churned. The analysis runs on the Python backend and the results appear in seven sections.

  1. Clean the dataFix types, remove duplicates and identifiers, fill missing values.
  2. Explore churn patternsChurn rate by contract, service and payment method.
  3. Train and compare modelsLogistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting.
  4. Score every customerChurn probability with High, Medium or Low risk.

The sample is the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset with 7,043 customers.
